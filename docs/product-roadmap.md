# Product roadmap intent

Long-term intent only: DinkusKit may offer three subscription tiers whose
primary differentiator is the included number of sites and/or Inventory pools.
That is an incentive direction, not a billing design or a promise of prices,
limits, or availability.

Still undecided:

- tier names, prices, currency, trial treatment, and billing provider;
- exact site and pool counts, whether the allowances are separate or combined,
  and whether overages exist;
- eligibility, upgrades, downgrades, grandfathering, pauses, cancellation,
  refunds, and enforcement timing;
- whether user seats or member roles are quota dimensions;
- whether Commerce, Inventory, or another service changes tier eligibility;
- how usage is measured when a site is pending, failed, revoked, or shared;
- entitlement ownership and the operator/customer support workflow.

The merchant account remains the owner of sites and pools, regardless of which
login user is acting. It has exactly one merchant-scoped Owner/root; employees
may receive explicitly selected privileges, but this does not decide whether
seats or roles count toward a future tier. Subscription work must not redefine
that ownership or the separate EmDash editor identity. No billing framework,
checkout, quota enforcement, seat policy, pricing copy, or subscription runtime
is part of this roadmap record.
