# DinkusKit.com design system

Status: layout (L3 page + L5 header), color, type and effects locked; brand images in;
home (H4) and Start your store (S9 with S6's plugin row) picked in round 7 and built. Each value gets locked in its own
GrillTrack round (layout, then palette, then type) per `emdash-stitch-design`.
The green look approved on 30 Sep 2026 is replaced by the brand palette above.

## Who it is for

EmDash site owners who want to sell, and the agents that help them. The site
explains DinkusKit (Commerce, Payments, Ship plugins on the EmDash Registry
plus hosted services), gets an owner to `/start`, and hosts the docs.

## Atmosphere

- Density: 4 (balanced; generous whitespace, nothing cramped).
- Variance: 3 to 5 (mostly a calm grid; room for one offset hero).
- Mood: workshop-honest, quiet confidence, plain shop words. A tool built in
  the open, not a SaaS billboard.

## Type (LOCKED round 3, 23 Oct 2026: Stitch F1)

Generated by Stitch (generate_variants, TEXT_FONT) on the locked page.

- Display: Space Grotesk 600/700 for h1 to h3 and the wordmark (`--font-display`).
- Body: Inter 400/500/600 (`--font-body`).
- Labels: Space Mono 700 for eyebrows and the wordmark's asterisks (`--font-label`).
- Served from the site through `@fontsource/*` packages, never a font CDN.
- h1 `clamp(2.5rem, 4.6vw, 3.9rem)`; body copy stays 60 to 70ch.

## Effects (LOCKED round 4, 23 Oct 2026: version B)

From `emdash-surface-fx` (assets vendored unchanged in `src/fx/vendor`, mounted
by `src/fx/surface-fx.ts`). Ronald picked B over the A blend ("new version is
my favorite").

- One dithered Bayer wash fixed behind every CMS page, recolored from emerald
  (`--focus`, top left) to ultraviolet (`--mark`, bottom right), opacity .26,
  faded out under the header. Paused off screen by the viewport pool; a still
  frame when the visitor asks for less motion.
- Topic cards on split pages carry a faint emerald dither (the same Bayer
  tile) and, for a mouse only, lean up to 4 degrees and glow as the mouse comes
  within 160px. Phones and reduced motion get flat cards.
- No scroll or cursor hijacking; pointer effects stay card-local CSS variables.

## Hard rules

- Every page is a CMS page (`pages` collection, `layout` blocks). A new
  section is a new block type in `seed/seed.json`, never a code-only route.
- Keep `data-*` hooks, accessible names and copy that tests pin.
- No emojis in chrome, no made-up numbers, no buttons for features that are
  not live.
- No horizontal scroll at 320 to 767 px.
